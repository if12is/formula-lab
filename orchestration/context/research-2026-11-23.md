## N-09 methanol (CAS 67-56-1): sourced, rule NOT changed
1. REACH Annex XVII entry 69 (Reg. (EU) 2018/589, Annex), https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2018/589/annex, read 2026-11-23, L3:
   "Methanol CAS No 67-56-1 ... Shall not be placed on the market to the general public after 9 May 2018 in windscreen washing or
   defrosting fluids, in a concentration equal to or greater than 0,6 % by weight." Scope is windscreen washing/defrosting fluids ONLY.
   It does NOT cover fine fragrance or air fresheners, so the round 1 lead (0.6% for fragrances) is wrong for this product scope.
2. Reg. (EC) 1223/2009 Annex III entry 52, https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1223/annex/III, read 2026-11-23, L3:
   "Methanol / Methyl alcohol / 67-56-1 / 200-659-6 / Denaturant for ethanol and isopropyl alcohol / 5 % (as a % ethanol and isopropyl alcohol)".
3. Annex II (prohibited): full text read, no entry for methanol itself (only "reaction product of acetophenone, formaldehyde, cyclohexylamine,
   methanol and acetic acid", EC 406-230-1, which is not 67-56-1).
Caveat: this is the retained text as of 31 Dec 2020; later EU amendments (EUR-Lex current consolidated version) could not be read.
Decision: the sourced cosmetics limit is a ratio to the ETHANOL + ISOPROPANOL content (5 %), not a %w/w of the finished product, and the DSL
(`packages/engine/src/compliance/dsl.ts`) only expresses substance limits as %w/w of the product. Writing 5 % w/w would be wrong, and a
%w/w proxy would be invented. The engine also cannot say anything for air fresheners (not cosmetics; entry 69 does not apply). So
`methanol-fragrance.json` stays draft, `source: null`, `pendingSource: true`, blanket `forbidden`, and the tests are unchanged. Next:
owner decides (a) a DSL basis "percent of another substance group" for Annex III style limits, (b) whether the blanket ban stays for air
fresheners with no regulatory source. Note: the blanket ban in the plan has NO basis in either text for fine fragrance.

## Egypt VAT (Law 67/2016, ETA English translation "Updated on: January 15, 2023"): sourced, rates NOT changed
URL: https://eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2024-02/Law-english-no.67-2016.pdf.pdf, read 2026-11-23, L3 (text is the ETA translation).
- Article 3: "Standard VAT rate on goods and services shall be (13%) for fiscal year 2016/2017, and shall be (14%) as of the beginning of
  fiscal year 2017/2018 ... Such goods and services exported ... shall be zero-rated." So 14 and 0 (export) are legally grounded.
- Table, "First - subject only to Table Tax", item 10: "Soap and synthetic detergents for household use ... Value (5%)".
- Table, "Second: subject to VAT and Table Tax", item 5: "Perfume & moistening products, cosmetics, health and hair-care products ... Value (8%)".
  Value = selling price to the end consumer.
DISCREPANCY for the owner and accountant: per this text, household soap and detergents carry a 5% Table Tax and no VAT line, and cosmetics carry
VAT 14% plus 8% Table Tax. The project assumption (14% VAT for detergents) is not what this text says; amendments after Jan 2023 were not
checked. So `config/tax.php` gets a `legal_reference` block only; allowed rates, regime confidence (L6) and `pending` are unchanged.
