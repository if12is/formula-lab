# OIML R 22 source record (C-08)

Status: RESOLVED for 20 C (retrieved 2026-11-21). Confidence L3_standard.

- Document: OIML R 22 (1975), International alcoholometric tables, https://www.oiml.org/en/files/pdf_r/r022-e75.pdf (scanned, 71 pages; PDF page = printed page).
- Method and formula: pp.4-5 (rho20 = A1 + sum_{k=2..12} A_k p^(k-1), p = mass fraction decimal; q = rho20(p)/rho20(100%) * p; pure ethanol 789.24 kg/m3), p.12 (full formula, validity -20..+40 C).
- Coefficients A_k, B_k, C_ik: p.13, transcribed by eye from the scan into packages/engine/data/oiml-r22/coefficients.json. Only A_k are used (t = 20 C); they are verified because the generated values match 50 printed points of Table IIIa (pp.44-45) and Table IIIb (pp.48-49). B_k and C_ik are NOT verified or used.
- table-20c.json: generated by packages/engine/scripts/gen-oiml-r22.ts, 0.1 %w/w grid, 10 decimals. rho_ethanol(20 C) from the formula = 789.2391233 (printed 789.24).
- Tests: alcohol.test.ts, 50 points (p = 0,2,...,98 %mass) copied from printed Tables IIIa/IIIb, plus 3 fractional points and p = 100.
- Not covered: temperatures other than 20 C (Tables I, II), Tables IVa/IVb and V were not compared.
